What is in it

Make every hit feel like a hit. Hitstop is an arcade-style combat feedback pack for brawlers, action games, platformers and fighting games: punches and kicks in light / medium / heavy tiers, critical and counter hits, swings and misses, blocks, parries, guard breaks, body falls, wall bounces, dashes, dodges, hit-stop accents, a rising combo counter, KO hits and fight UI. Listen to the 41-second demo and try the free 27-sound Lite first.

What's inside: 147 sounds, every one as WAV + OGG (294 files)

  • Punches (18): punch_light ×6, punch_medium ×6, punch_heavy ×6
  • Kicks (10): kick_light ×5, kick_heavy ×5
  • Critical hits (10): hit_critical ×5, hit_counter ×5
  • Swings & misses (15): whoosh_light ×5, whoosh_heavy ×5, whiff_miss ×5
  • Block & parry (14): block_guard ×5, parry_clang ×5, guard_break ×4
  • Body impacts (14): body_fall ×5, ground_slam ×5, wall_bounce ×4
  • Movement (18): dash ×5, dodge ×5, jump_launch ×4, land ×4
  • Game feel (17): hitstop_freeze ×5, combo_step 01-08 (one note higher per hit), ko_hit ×4
  • Layered heavy hit (20): 5 big hits, each as heavy_hit_full plus separate whoosh / impact / sweetener stems
  • Fight UI (11): ui_hp_low ×4 (heartbeat), ui_super_ready ×4, ui_round_start ×3

Built for game feel

  • 4 intensity tiers with stepped loudness (light about -22 LUFS, medium -19, heavy and critical -16), so you can map damage straight to sound
  • 4-6 variations of every hit, so a 10-hit combo doesn't sound copy-pasted
  • Layered stems: start the whoosh on the wind-up, fire impact + sweetener on the frame the hit connects (impact timings listed in the README)
  • 8-step combo counter in A minor pentatonic: play step 1, 2, 3... on each hit of a combo
  • Hits land on the frame: every sound starts within 5 ms, no silent lead-in, clean tails
  • Reads on small speakers: every impact keeps at least 25% of its energy above 300 Hz, so punches still land on laptops and phones; high frequencies kept smooth (under 10% of the energy above 8 kHz)
  • True peak ≤ -1 dBTP, 0 clipped samples; 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV + OGG Vorbis, same file names in both; QA report in the pack

Godot / Unity ready: use the OGG files directly in Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Unreal, Defold, RPG Maker or Construct. Tip: pick a random variant per hit and add ±1-2 semitones of random pitch.
