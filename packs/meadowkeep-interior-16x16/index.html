What is in it

588 tiles · 575 static + 13 animated (34 frames) · 6 autotile sets · 28 OGG sounds · Godot 4 + Tiled tested

Furnish a whole house in an afternoon. Meadowkeep Interior is a 16x16 top-down interior tileset with walls that autotile, furniture in matching wood and fabric colours, animated fireplaces, candles and rain windows - and the sound effects to go with them.

What's inside (588 tiles: 575 static + 13 animated, 34 animation frames)

  • Autotile: 6 complete 47-tile blob sets - stone, timber and brick wall caps + red, blue and teal rugs (282 tiles)
  • Floors: 30 tiles - oak, walnut, honey and white planks, checker tiles, carpets, stone, bathroom tiles
  • Walls: plaster, sage, sky, sand, 3 wainscot styles, brick, stone block and walnut panelling faces
  • Doors and windows in 4 woods: day, night, curtains, open/closed doors, an animated rain window
  • Living room: 3-seat sofas and loveseats in 5 fabrics, armchairs, fireplace (animated), TV stand (animated), piano, aquarium (animated)
  • Bedroom: double beds (5 fabrics x 2 woods + white), nightstands, wardrobe, dresser
  • Study: tall 1x2 bookshelf, low 2x1 bookshelf, tables, chairs in 4 directions, stools
  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stove + an animated steaming pot, fridge, wall cabinets
  • Bathroom: bathtub, toilet, washbasin, bath mats
  • Plants, floor and table lamps, wall sconce (animated), wall clock (animated), paintings, mirror, shelves
  • Table-top decor: candle (animated), vase, books, mug, small lamp, small plant

Animation built in - Tiled tile animations and Godot 4 TileSet animation frames are already set up. Drop the tiles in and they move.

Autotile built in - Tiled wangsets and a Godot 4 terrain set (match corners and sides) with collision on wall caps.

Sound effects included - 28 OGG sounds picked from our own sound packs: fireplace crackle and flares, rain and thunder heard from indoors, wind, night ambience, door creaks and knocks.

Tested before release - In Godot 4.3 and 4.7.2 we painted 29,000+ cells with set_cells_terrain_connect and every one got the expected tile; all 28 sounds loaded and played; captured frames of the example scene matched our reference render pixel for pixel. Tiled 1.11 loaded the map, read back all animations and wangsets, and rendered the map pixel-identical to the reference. The logs are in the pack.

Files: tilesheet PNG (1x + 4x), every tile as a single PNG, tile index (TXT + JSON), multi-tile object list (JSON), Tiled.tsx + example.tmx, Godot 4 TileSet.tres + example scene + test project, palette (.hex/.gpl/.png), OGG sounds.

Engines: Godot 4.3+ and Tiled ready; the PNG sheet works in Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker or any engine that reads a 16px grid.

Goes with: Meadowkeep Tileset (outdoor), Meadowkeep Heroes & Monsters, Meadowkeep UI Icons - same palette family and outline style.
