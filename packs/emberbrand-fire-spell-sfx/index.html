What is in it

Give your fire mage a full spellbook of sound. Emberbrand is 198 fire spell sounds for RPGs, roguelikes, action, tower-defense and strategy games, organised by the life of a spell: cast charge-ups, releases, projectile fly-bys, impacts in three sizes, five explosion tiers (up to meteor strike and chain explosion), ignite and burn-over-time ticks, fire walls and flame auras, extinguish and fizzle, a 6-step combo ladder, UI-ready cues and 15 seamless sustain loops. 5 variations of most sounds.

Built around the life of a fire spell, not a random folder of whooshes

  • Charge → release → fly → hit: cast_charge_short / cast_charge_long peak at the end so they chain straight into fireball_release, flame_burst or fire_dart_release; the fireball_fly loop or a panned fireball_flyby carries it; it lands as an impact or explosion, or burns out with fireball_dissipate.
  • Hits that scale: impact_small (-20 LUFS) → impact_medium (-18) → impact_large (-16), then explosion tiers small / medium / large plus meteor_strike and chain_explosion. A bigger hit is a louder file, no mixer work. EmberSfx.impact(power) picks the tier from a 0..1 value.
  • Burn damage over time: ignite → quiet burn_tick (-24 LUFS, made to play every second without wearing ears out) and burn_tick_heavy for crits → burn_spread → burn_out.
  • Walls, auras and the counter-play: fire_wall_rise / fire_wall_collapse, flame_aura_on / flame_aura_off, and extinguish, steam hiss, smother and cast fizzle for water, ice and failed casts.
  • Combo ladder + UI cues: fire_combo_1 … fire_combo_6 climb D major pentatonic (EmberSfx.combo(step)); cooldown_ready, skill_learned and ember_pickup for the spell bar. All tonal parts are in D, the same key as my Mendsong healing pack, so a fire mage and a healer layer without clashing.
  • 15 seamless loops (12 s, sample-exact, WAV loop points): a fireball in flight, flame aura, wall of fire, burning ground and a held full charge; loop_start / loop_stop fade them in and out.

Made for game feel

  • Hot and clear, not muddy: roaring flame bodies, crackling sparks and whooshing licks with the low end kept tight, so fire reads on laptop and phone speakers as well as headphones
  • Snappy: every one-shot starts within 5 ms of the file start (no silent lead-in) and ends with a clean tail; the variations of a sound match within 0.1 dB
  • True peak ≤ -1 dBTP, 0 clipped samples; 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV + OGG Vorbis, same file names in both; QA report with the measurement of every file

Godot 4 + Unity drop-in: copy ogg/ to res://emberbrand/ and add ember_sfx.gd as an Autoload named EmberSfx, or copy it to Assets/Resources/Emberbrand/ and add the EmberSfx component in Unity. Then EmberSfx.play("fireball_release") / EmberSfx.Play("fireball_release") plays a random variant (never the same one twice in a row); plus impact, combo, aura, loop_start / loop_stop and play_at / PlayAt for positional sounds. The Godot helper was tested headless in Godot 4.3 (every file loads). File names are plain lowercase snake_case, so GameMaker, Unreal, Defold, RPG Maker, Construct or Phaser work too.
