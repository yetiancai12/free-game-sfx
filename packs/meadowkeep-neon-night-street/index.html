What is in it

A rainy night city street in 16x16: wet asphalt with neon reflections, blob-47 roads and sidewalks, curbs, puddles, road markings, small shopfronts, a roof + facade building shell, street lamps with dithered light pools and five flickering neon icon signs (no text). Tested in Godot 4.3 / 4.7.2 and Tiled 1.12.2.

What's inside

  • 274 tiles = 265 static + 9 animated objects (32 animation frames listed separately; 297 unique ids incl. frames)
  • 3 complete blob-47 autotile sets: asphalt road, sidewalk, wet asphalt (Tiled wangsets + Godot terrains)
  • 8 stackable wet/dry transition overlays, 10 curb/gutter tiles, 6 puddles + animated puddle shimmer, 14 road markings
  • Building shell: 13 roof pieces (incl. 4 inner corners) + 5 facade pieces; roofs at least 2 cells thick
  • 8 street objects / 9 states (shopfront 3x3 closed/open, wall pipe 1x3, AC unit, trash bin, electric cabinet 1x2, road barrier 2x1, bollard, plastic stool)
  • Street lamp 1x2 + wall lamp + 2 ground light pools (dithered, palette only)
  • 5 neon icon signs (bowl, cup, key, star, potion) with 2-4 frame flicker; 3 wet-asphalt shimmers
  • Meadowkeep-Neon-64 palette (.hex/.gpl/PNG), atlas 1x/4x + index, single PNGs
  • Tiled example map (32x20, 14 layers) and Godot 4 example project with tests
  • No audio. No Unity project.

Engines

Tested: Godot 4.3.stable, Godot 4.7.2.stable, Tiled 1.12.2. Unity / GameMaker / RPG Maker / Aseprite can load the 16x16 PNGs and atlas; no project files for them are included.
