What is in it

Give every story beat its moment. Story Beats is a sound pack for visual novels, dating sims and story games: heart-flutter and blush stingers, the "wait... I get it" realization, shock hits, sad falls, comedy boings, sweat drops and bonks, tension rises, relief, affection up/down, a "they will remember that" choice cue, route and ending unlocks, achievements, chapter title cards, and scene transitions (time skip, flashback in/out, dream in/out, day to night, location change) plus CG reveal shimmers. Every cue has 4-5 variations and the big moments come in short and long versions.

Made for storytelling

  • One key family: every note and chord in the tonal cues uses the C major / A minor note set (white keys), so cues fit each other and sit over BGM in C major / A minor or nearby keys. Comedy hits, heartbeats and whooshes are effects not tied to a key
  • Loudness by role: transitions and quiet reactions about -20 LUFS (under dialogue), stingers -18 LUFS, unlocks and chapter cards -16 LUFS. Variations of one cue match within 0.1 dB
  • Lands on the line: every sound starts within 5 ms (no silent lead-in) and ends with a clean tail
  • Clear on phones and laptops: energy kept above 300 Hz and smooth highs (under 10% above 8 kHz); reverb cues in real stereo
  • True peak ≤ -1 dBTP, 0 clipped samples; 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV + OGG Vorbis, same file names in both; QA report with the measurement of every file in the pack

Ren'Py drop-in: copy the ogg/ folder to game/vn_stingers/ and vn_stingers.rpy into game/. Then one line per story beat: $ vn_sting("shock_hit") plays a random variant (never the same one twice in a row), $ vn_sting("romance") picks from a mood group, $ vn_scene_cue("location_swoosh", "relief") plays a transition and a mood stinger together, and every file also has a normal name such as play sound vns_shock_hit_01. Stingers get their own channel on the Sound volume slider, so they can overlap a transition.

Godot / Unity / any engine: lowercase snake_case file names numbered _01.._05 that import cleanly into Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Unreal, RPG Maker, TyranoBuilder or Naninovel. Use the OGG files in-engine, keep the WAVs for editing.
