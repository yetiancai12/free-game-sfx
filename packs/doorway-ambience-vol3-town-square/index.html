What is in it

Step into the square. Fountain spray, market cloth, a guild door, the clock tower, a side alley: each scene in three places to stand. The three loops of a scene share one timeline, so crossfading through a doorway never jumps.

What's inside (exact counts)

  • 5 scenes x 3 perspectives = 15 loops: Fountain, Market Stall, Guild Door, Clock Tower, Alley
  • 1 panorama bed: the full town square from the centre
  • 36 accents: clock chime, pigeon flurry, crate thud, market bell (3 variations x 3 positions)
  • 52 sounds in total (WAV, OGG and MP3 versions of the same stem count as one)
  • Two paid downloads on Ko-fi: WAV 48 kHz / 24-bit with smpl loop points, or a lighter MP3 + OGG pack
  • Loudness: -20 LUFS loops, -18 LUFS accents, true peak at or below -1 dBTP

Why this pack

  • Town theme, not weather: pairs with Doorway Ambience Vol.2 without overlapping scenes
  • Synced perspectives: outside / indoors / distant, same length to the sample
  • Engine-ready: circular loops plus WAV smpl loop points for Godot, Unity, GameMaker and FMOD
