What is in it

Give your ice mage a full spellbook of sound. Frostbite is 203 ice spell sounds for RPGs, roguelikes, action, tower-defense and strategy games, organised by the life of a spell: cast charge-ups, releases and a frost nova, projectile fly-bys, freeze impacts in three sizes, five shatter tiers (up to ice meteor and chain shatter), freeze and frostbite damage-over-time ticks, ice walls and ice shields, thaw and fizzle, a 6-step combo ladder, UI-ready cues and 15 seamless sustain loops. 5 variations of most sounds.

Built around the life of an ice spell, not a random folder of crunches

  • Charge → release → fly → hit: cast_charge_short / cast_charge_long peak at the end so they chain straight into ice_lance_release, frostbolt_release, ice_shard_release or a radial frost_nova; crystal_form marks the spell as ready; a panned ice_lance_flyby / ice_shard_flyby carries it; it lands as an impact or a shatter, or fades out with frostbolt_dissipate.
  • Hits that scale: impact_small (-20 LUFS) → impact_medium (-18) → impact_large (-16), then shatter tiers shatter_small (-18) → shatter_medium / shatter_large (-16) plus ice_meteor and chain_shatter. A bigger hit is a louder file, no mixer work. FrostSfx.impact(power) picks small / medium / large from a 0..1 value; FrostSfx.impact_legacy(power) spans all six impact and shatter tiers.
  • Freeze damage over time: freeze_onset → quiet frostbite_tick (-24 LUFS, made to play every second without wearing ears out) and frostbite_tick_heavy (-20) for crits → frost_spread (pans to a neighbour) → thaw_out.
  • Walls, shields and the counter-play: ice_wall_rise → ice_wall loop → ice_wall_collapse; ice_shield_on → ice_shield loop → ice_shield_off; and thaw, melt_drip, frost_break and cast_fizzle for fire meeting ice and for failed casts.
  • Combo ladder + UI cues: ice_combo_1 … ice_combo_6 climb A minor pentatonic (FrostSfx.combo(step); step 6 is a chord); cooldown_ready, skill_learned and frost_orb_pickup for the spell bar. All tonal parts (crystal chimes, power hum) are in A minor pentatonic, a cool partner to my Emberbrand fire pack and Mendsong healing pack.
  • 15 seamless loops (12 s, sample-exact, WAV loop points): blizzard, ice shield, ice wall, frozen ground and a held full charge; loop_start / loop_stop fade them in and out.

Made for game feel

  • Cold and glassy, not harsh: crystalline cracks, glassy crystal tones and icy wind with the low end kept tight, so ice reads on laptop and phone speakers as well as headphones
  • Snappy: every one-shot starts within 5 ms of the file start (no silent lead-in) and ends with a clean tail; the variations of a one-shot match within 0.1 dB
  • True peak ≤ -1 dBTP, 0 clipped samples; 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV + OGG Vorbis, same file names in both; every file measured (length, loudness, peak listed in MANIFEST.csv)

Godot 4 + Unity drop-in: copy ogg/ to res://frostbite/ and add frost_sfx.gd as an Autoload named FrostSfx, or copy it to Assets/Resources/Frostbite/ and add the FrostSfx component in Unity. Then FrostSfx.play("frostbolt_release") / FrostSfx.Play("frostbolt_release") plays a random variant (never the same one twice in a row); plus impact, combo, aura, cast / release, loop_start / loop_stop and play_at / PlayAt for positional sounds. An optional IceSfx alias (ice_sfx.gd / IceSfx.cs) forwards the same calls. The Godot helper was tested headless in Godot 4.7 (every file loads, every helper call runs); the Unity script builds with 0 errors against a UnityEngine stub (not yet run inside the Unity editor). File names are plain lowercase snake_case, so GameMaker, Unreal, Defold, RPG Maker, Construct or Phaser work too.
