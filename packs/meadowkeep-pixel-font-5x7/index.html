What is in it

191 characters · 2 weights · TTF + OTF + WOFF

A crisp 5x7 pixel font for retro and cozy games — HP bars, dialogue boxes, menus, item tooltips and score counters.

  • 2 weights: Regular and Bold
  • 191 characters: full printable ASCII + Latin-1 Supplement (À-ÿ, ß Æ Ø Ñ Ç, ¿ ¡ « » £ ¥ © ® ° ± ½ …) — English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Nordic
