What is in it

Give your earth mage a full spellbook of sound. Terrastone is 198 earth and rock spell sounds for RPGs, roguelikes, action, tower-defense and strategy games, organised by the life of a spell: cast charge-ups, stone spear, boulder and pebble releases, projectile fly-bys, rock impacts in three sizes, five quake and rockfall tiers (up to rockfall and chain quake), bury and crush damage-over-time ticks, rock walls and earth aura (stone skin), crumble and fizzle, a 6-step combo ladder, UI-ready cues and 15 seamless sustain loops. 5 variations of most sounds.

Built around the life of an earth spell, not a random folder of rock crunches

  • Charge → release → fly → hit: cast_charge_short / cast_charge_long peak at the end so they chain straight into stone_spear_release, boulder_release or pebble_dart_release; gravel_form marks the spell as ready; a panned stone_spear_flyby / pebble_dart_flyby carries it; it lands as a rock impact or a quake, or crumbles mid-air with boulder_dissipate.
  • Hits that scale: impact_small (-20 LUFS) → impact_medium (-18) → impact_large (-16), then quake tiers quake_small (-18) → quake_medium / quake_large (-16) plus rockfall and chain_quake. A bigger hit is a louder file, no mixer work. EarthSfx.impact(power) picks small / medium / large from a 0..1 value.
  • Bury & crush damage over time: bury_onset → quiet crush_tick (-24 LUFS, made to play every second without wearing ears out) and crush_tick_heavy (-20) for crits → crush_spread (pans to a neighbour) → crumble_out.
  • Walls, stone skin and the counter-play: rock_wall_rise → rock_wall loop → rock_wall_collapse; earth_aura_on → earth_aura loop → earth_aura_off; and crumble, sand_scatter, rock_break and cast_fizzle for broken spells and failed casts.
  • Combo ladder + UI cues: earth_combo_1 … earth_combo_6 climb D major pentatonic (EarthSfx.combo(step); step 6 is a chord); cooldown_ready, skill_learned and stone_pickup for the spell bar and ore/mana pickups.
  • 15 seamless loops (12 s, sample-exact, WAV loop points): debris field, earth aura, rock wall, cracked ground and a held full charge; loop_start / loop_stop fade them in and out.

Made for game feel

  • Heavy but clean: gravel and dust bodies, stone cracks and low sine-sweep thumps with the sub kept tight, so rock reads on laptop and phone speakers as well as headphones
  • Snappy: every one-shot starts within 5 ms of the file start (no silent lead-in) and ends with a clean tail; the variations of a one-shot match within 0.1 dB
  • True peak ≤ -1 dBTP, 0 clipped samples; 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV + OGG Vorbis, same file names in both; every file measured (length, loudness, peak listed in MANIFEST.csv)

Godot 4 + Unity drop-in: copy ogg/ to res://terrastone/ and add earth_sfx.gd as an Autoload named EarthSfx, or copy it to Assets/Resources/Terrastone/ and add the EarthSfx component in Unity. Then EarthSfx.play("boulder_release") / EarthSfx.Play("boulder_release") plays a random variant (never the same one twice in a row); plus impact, combo, aura, cast / release, loop / loop_start / loop_stop and play_at / PlayAt for positional sounds. The Godot helper was tested headless in Godot 4.7 (every file loads, every helper call runs); the Unity script builds with 0 errors against a UnityEngine stub (not yet run inside the Unity editor). File names are plain lowercase snake_case, so GameMaker, Unreal, Defold, RPG Maker, Construct or Phaser work too.

Part of my elemental spell line: pair Terrastone with Emberbrand (fire), Frostbite (ice), Stormcaller (lightning) and Mendsong (healing) for a full party of casters. The Godot/Unity helpers use the same calls (impact(power), combo(step), aura(on), cast() / release(), loop()), and the tonal parts share D major pentatonic with Emberbrand and Mendsong, so an earth combo, a fireball and a heal sit in the same key.
