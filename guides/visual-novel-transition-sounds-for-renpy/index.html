Visual novel stingers and transition sounds for Ren'Py

Story Beats is a sound pack for visual novels, dating sims and story games: romance and blush stingers, realization hits, shock, sad falls, comedy boings, tension and relief, route and ending unlocks, chapter title cards, scene transitions and CG reveals. The full pack has 175 sounds and a Ren'Py drop-in file; the free Lite has 28.

Tip from the listing: copy the ogg folder into your game folder and the .rpy file next to it, then trigger one cue per story beat with a single line of Ren'Py script.
