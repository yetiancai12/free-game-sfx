Free hit and impact sound effects for Godot and Unity

Two combat-feel packs, each with a free Lite version. Hitstop is an arcade combat feedback pack: punches and kicks in light / medium / heavy tiers, critical and counter hits, swings and misses, blocks, parries, guard breaks, a rising combo counter and KO hits. Quakehammer is built around weight: thumps, ground slams, booms and screen-shake layers in three force tiers, with Godot 4 and Unity helper scripts in the full pack.

Tip from the Hitstop listing: pick a random variant per hit and add 1-2 semitones of random pitch so a long combo does not sound copy-pasted.
