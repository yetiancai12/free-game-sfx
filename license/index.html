License summary

This page restates, in one place, the license wording shown on each store listing. It is a summary, not a legal document: the LICENSE.txt inside every download is the authoritative text.

The pixel art packs allow commercial use in games and do not allow reselling or redistributing the files; several also say not for training machine-learning models.
