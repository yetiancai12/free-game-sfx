About this library

This is a free, searchable catalog of the game sound effect, ambience, music and 16x16 pixel art packs made by yetiancai. It has no shop of its own: every pack page links to the free Lite version (where one exists) and to the store pages on itch.io, Gumroad, Ko-fi and Fab, where downloads and payments are handled.

Where the information comes from

Descriptions, counts, formats and license wording are taken from the live store listings and were last checked on 2026-11-11. If a number is not on a listing, it is not on this site. Prices change on the stores; this site only shows the lowest listed starting price ("from") and you should confirm it on the store page.

Preview audio

Previews are YouTube videos embedded only after you click play, so loading a page sets no YouTube cookies. No audio files are hosted here.

Privacy

No analytics, no ads and no cookies are set by this site.
